Privacy & analytics

Last updated: October 22, 2026.

This portfolio runs on GitHub Pages. It does not require an account or ask visitors to submit sensitive personal information. Links to email or WhatsApp open your own client/application.

Optional analytics

Google Analytics 4 and Microsoft Clarity may be enabled to understand aggregate visits, referral sources, devices, engagement, heatmaps and session interactions. Neither provider is loaded by the optional integration until a visitor consents. If the site is not configured with provider IDs, no optional analytics runs.

Analytics may collect usage, device/browser information, approximate location and interaction events in accordance with the provider's privacy controls. Clarity session insights may capture page interactions. The site owner should review masking settings and never collect sensitive content.

Events

When enabled and accepted, the portfolio may record project views, filter choices, CV link clicks, contacts opened via email/WhatsApp, language selections and page-scroll milestones. Clicking a contact link is only an intention signal, not proof that an email was delivered or that a qualified lead was generated.

Consent and choices

To control optional analytics, choose Allow analytics or Keep private in the consent notice. Your preference is saved in your browser. You can change it using Privacy settings in the portfolio footer. Withdrawing consent refreshes the page without loading analytics again. Browser privacy controls and ad blockers may also prevent collection.

What can't be identified

Aggregate analytics does not reveal visitor names, personal email addresses, or exactly who visited. Identifying a potential client requires voluntary contact.

Third parties and contact

GitHub Pages delivers the site. If enabled with consent, Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity process analytics data according to their own policies. For privacy questions contact mykholys30@gmail.com.

Current integration: Google Analytics 4 and Microsoft Clarity are configured for this portfolio. Both are optional and do not load until a visitor grants consent. Advertising storage is not enabled by this consent choice. Statistics may be incomplete because of consent choices and browser privacy settings.
